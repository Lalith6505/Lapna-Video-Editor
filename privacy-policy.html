Privacy Policy for Lapna Video Editor Pro

Effective Date: October 11, 2026
Application: Lapna Video Editor Pro (com.lapna.videoeditor)
Developer: Eng. Lalith Prasanna Madanayake (Sisaara Engineers Private Limited)

1. Overview & Commitment

Lapna Video Editor Pro is built as an offline-capable, privacy-respecting video editing suite. We firmly believe that your creative media, personal footage, audio tracks, and project drafts belong entirely to you. All video processing, timeline rendering, trimming, and color grading happen locally on your Android device.

2. Data Collection & Processing

  • No Media Uploads: Lapna never uploads your videos, photos, or audio recordings to external cloud servers. All rendering uses on-device hardware acceleration.
  • No Personal Information Harvesting: We do not collect names, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS coordinates, or biometric facial tracking data.
  • No Invasive Advertising / Tracking: Lapna Video Editor Pro is a premium creative utility free of third-party advertising SDKs and tracking brokers.

3. Android Permissions Required & Justification

  • Read Media Videos / Audio / Images: Needed exclusively to let you browse and select the video clips and music tracks you wish to edit in the timeline.
  • Storage Access: Needed to export and save your rendered 1080p/4K MP4 video files and project JSON backups directly to your device storage.
  • Internet Permission: Used only when you explicitly tap to share an exported video through external applications (e.g. YouTube, WhatsApp, Instagram) or when viewing the developer contact page.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
